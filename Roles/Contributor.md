# Contributors

Contributors are community members who participate in AOSSIE projects by writing code, fixing bugs, improving documentation, submitting feature ideas, or reviewing issues and pull requests.

## Eligibility to be a Contributor

Anyone is eligible and welcome to become an AOSSIE contributor! There are no prerequisites other than a desire to learn, collaborate, and contribute positively to open-source software.

## Responsibilities of a Contributor

As a Contributor, you should:
* Abide by AOSSIE's [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [AI Usage Policy](../Rules/AI.md).
* Read and follow the repository's [Contribution Guidelines](../Rules/Contribution.md) and pull request templates.
* Submit well-documented issues and pull requests that address real project needs.
* Respond constructively to review feedback from triageurs, mentors, and maintainers.
* Test your changes locally before submitting pull requests.

## Privileges and Recognition

As an active Contributor, you:
* Have your code and documentation contributions recognized across repository history and credits.
* May request triage or write permissions to the repository from AOSSIE Maintainers and Admins after demonstrating a consistent track record.
* Can suggest issues and pull requests for inclusion in project milestones during planning.
* Are eligible for recognition and promotion to advanced community roles such as [Triageur](Triageurs.md), [Ideator](Ideator.md), [Mentor](Mentors.md), or [Maintainer](Maintainer.md).

## Communication

* Use official AOSSIE communication channels (Discord and GitHub) to ask questions, discuss issues, and seek guidance.
* Engage respectfully with all community members.
