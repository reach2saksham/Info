# Ideators

Ideators are community members, maintainers, mentors, researchers, and open-source contributors who propose, refine, and document project ideas for a particular AOSSIE project. They contribute during the ideation and planning phase by identifying meaningful features, enhancements, and project concepts, and by documenting them for community discussion and evaluation.

The role of an Ideator is focused on the idea generation and planning phase. Once a project idea is accepted and documented, its implementation and mentorship are handled by the designated maintainers and mentors, incorporating the suggestions and guidelines provided by the Ideator.

The Ideator role concludes once the idea has been accepted and documented. For most projects, however, Ideators also serve as maintainers, mentors, or contributors within the same repository, and any further involvement is in those capacities rather than as an Ideator.


## Eligibility to be an Ideator

To be recognised as an Ideator for a project, an individual should:

* Have a good understanding of the project's domain, technical ecosystem, AOSSIE rules and constraints.
* Actively participate in that project's community discussions and planning.
* Propose clear, feasible, and impactful project ideas, feature enhancements, or improvements.
* Where appropriate, draft well-structured project proposals describing the motivation, objectives, technical stack, scope, deliverables, and complete roadmap, for review by the community, maintainers, and mentors.
* Provide context for proposed ideas and address questions from maintainers, mentors, and prospective contributors during ideation phase.

## Process and Communication

* Share and discuss ideas publicly on AOSSIE Discord channels.
* Keep idea descriptions structured, realistic, and up-to-date.
