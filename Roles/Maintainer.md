# Maintainers

Maintainers are among the most established contributors in the community, responsible for the technical direction, code quality, repository lifecycle, and governance of specific projects within AOSSIE. They participate in making decisions regarding the strategy, priorities, and architectural evolution of the project.

## Eligibility to be a Maintainer

To be appointed as a Maintainer, an individual must:
* Have a proven track record of high-quality code and documentation contributions to the project.
* Demonstrate deep technical understanding of the repository's codebase and architecture, as well as broad knowledge across project components.
* Exhibit strong commitment to code reviews, community guidance, and open communication.
* Commit to using privileges responsibly for the good of the project.
* Be able to exercise judgment for the good of the project, independent of employers, personal affiliations, or external pressures.
* Be recommended by existing project maintainers and approved by organization admins.

## Responsibilities of a Maintainer

As a Maintainer, you should:
* Co-own the technical strategy, architecture, and development roadmap of the repository.
* Review, give constructive feedback on, and merge pull requests in a timely manner.
* Ensure that project contributions comply with AOSSIE's [Contribution Guidelines](../Rules/Contribution.md), [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and [AI Usage Policy](../Rules/AI.md).
* Clean up stale issues, pull requests, and outdated branches.
* Guide and support mentors, triageurs, and contributors working on the project.
* Maintain clear, updated project documentation and skill definitions.

## Communication and Conduct

* Use official AOSSIE discord channels for all project discussions and decision-making.
* Maintain transparency in technical evaluations, approvals, and architectural decisions.
* Avoid private or non-transparent decision-making that bypasses public community discussion.
* Represent the project in public and community forums with integrity and responsibility.

## Appointment Process

Maintainers are appointed by the Organization Admins based on the needs of the project and the individual's demonstrated technical expertise, sustained contributions, community involvement, and ability to lead and support the repository.
